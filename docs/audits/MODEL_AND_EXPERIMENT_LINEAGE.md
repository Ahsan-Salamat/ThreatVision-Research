## Lineage constraints

- MODEL-224 and MODEL-256 are separate artifacts with different hashes, input shapes, preprocessing orders, and independently defined input profiles. Never substitute one for the other.
- The recovered 4,000-row manifest counts hexadecimal filename/hash strings; it does not establish that underlying binaries or images remain available or authentic.
- The later 79,997 folder count is not evidence of a completed evaluation or of the source of either checkpoint.
- The approximately 85% recollection remains owner-reported. The anomalous 22-row log cannot be used to support it.
- The application and research preprocessing implementations now match exactly on three deterministic synthetic fixtures. This verifies implementation parity, not historical training parity.
